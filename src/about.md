<div class="prose">

Alice Hamilton Farmer is an international lawyer. For twenty years she has worked on
refugee protection and the rights of people in displacement, in the field and in the
courts.

Until January 2024 she was head of the United States legal team for **UNHCR**, the UN
Refugee Agency, where she oversaw strategic litigation and policy analysis, drafted the
agency's legal positions, and represented it before the US Supreme Court and the federal
courts of appeals. She was also UNHCR's focal point for climate change, and led its work on
access to counsel for people seeking asylum. Her field service with UNHCR includes
deployment as Senior Protection Officer in Chişinău, Moldova at the onset of the Ukrainian
refugee emergency; refugee status determination across the Caribbean; and protection
cluster lead in Liberia.

Before UNHCR she worked as an international human rights lawyer for **Human Rights Watch**,
the **ACLU**, and the **Norwegian Refugee Council**. At Human Rights Watch she created and
led the organisation's global campaign to end the immigration detention of children,
carrying out field investigations in more than twelve countries. Her research took her into
detention centres and refugee camps in Malta, Indonesia, Greece, Italy and Thailand, and
into public schools across the American South.

She now studies how climate change is altering the international legal framework of forced
displacement. She is a Fellow at the **Center for Advanced Study in the Behavioral
Sciences** at Stanford and was a Visiting Fellow at the **Refugee Studies Centre**,
University of Oxford. She is building a proof of concept for a climate change law lab, and
is co-editing *Climate Change, Migration, Gender, and the Law*, forthcoming from Edward
Elgar.

## Teaching

She has lectured on human rights at Harvard Law School, Georgetown University Law Center,
and the Institut d'études politiques de Paris (Sciences Po).

## Education

- JD, New York University School of Law
- MPA, Harvard Kennedy School of Government
- BA, Harvard College

## Other

She serves on the board of the Bromley Charitable Trust, an environmental philanthropic
foundation. She speaks French.
